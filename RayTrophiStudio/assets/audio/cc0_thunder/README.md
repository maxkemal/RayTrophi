# CC0 thunder variations

Seven different SFXMint thunder sounds, packaged as mono PCM16 WAV at the original
44.1 kHz rate. These are **publisher-generated audio (AI/procedural), not field
recordings**. Subjective listening acceptance in RayTrophi remains with the user.

- Source collection: https://sfxmint.com/sounds/ambience-thunder
- Publisher license: https://sfxmint.com/license
- License: CC0 1.0, including commercial use, modification and redistribution.
- Verified on 2026-10-12; full dedication is included in `CC0-1.0.txt`.

Each `.asset.json` carries the exact source page, author, license and tags. The
manifest records download URLs, original and packaged SHA256, PCM measurements,
channel conversion, leading-quiet trim and edge fades. Variation 08 had a quiet
source and received a documented constant gain; no dynamics compression was used.

The environment recipe chooses variations 06/07/08 for nearby events and
01/02/03/04 for distant events. These names are the publisher's intended character;
they do not replace audition in the final scene. No sound is a seamless loop.
All seven can also be previewed or dragged from Assets as ordinary scene sources.

In Scene Audio > Lightning / Thunder, **Connect CC0 thunder variations** installs
the asset bindings. The recipe does not create a storm or change visual lightning.
Its settings and imported assets are saved with the project; automatic thunder
uses the visual timeline clock and the existing storm-cell producer.
